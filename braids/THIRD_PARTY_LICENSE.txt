braids/build/braids.bin contains compiled code from Mutable Instruments
Braids and stmlib (https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack,
https://github.com/pichenettes/stmlib), built from:
  eurorack f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86
  stmlib   d18def816c51d1da0c108236928b2bbd25c17481
Files used: braids/macro_oscillator.cc, analog_oscillator.cc,
digital_oscillator.cc, resources.cc and their headers; stmlib/utils/random.cc,
dsp.h, random.h, stmlib.h. Their source is not in this repository; see
braids/build.sh.
